# Gridzilla website: design note

1. Direction: one product page for grid operators, judges and partners, read as a Dribbble-grade product launch (the owner's Vexo shot), not an "energy website". Black and green like the team's deck (owner, 25 Oct: "ours is black and green and super premium"), one theme, locked. Dials: variance 8, motion 6, density 3.
2. Borrowed from Vexo (Muhammad Shofiuddoula): the huge 40 px frame with a cool grey gradient inside, giant display type (we tried Vexo's uppercase; the owner picked sentence case), a dark portrait card at the centre of the frame (ours is the console in a thin dark bezel rising from the frame's bottom edge), the black pill with a round arrow badge.
3. Borrowed from 21st.dev: the inverted-curve notch of "Adaptive Notch Navigation Bar" (@arunachalam) for the nav tab cut into the hero frame; the spring count of "Number Ticker" (@dillionverma) for the proof numbers, changed to render the final value first so it is never empty; the sticky split of "Sticky Content Wrapper" (@hyperiux) for How it works, rebuilt with IntersectionObserver instead of GSAP.
4. Borrowed from Dribbble search "modern website": the small rounded video card at the hero's bottom right (Piqo Studio, Shofiuddoula's other shots) and the device rising out of a frame ("New era of finance", Phenomenon Studio).
5. Palette (locked, the deck's dark theme from dashboard/src/pitch/pitch.css): ground #0b0b0f, tile #1d1d1f at 92 % with an inner #2a2a2e hairline, quiet tile #141418, ink #f5f5f7, secondary #a1a1a6, tertiary #8e8e93, fill #2a2a2e, press #3a3a3e. Accent green #34c759 only on the live dot, link hover underlines, icons in the AI patch and the one proof number. Chart colours live only inside charts. Behind everything the deck's Mesh Gradient (21st.dev @paper-design, copied unchanged) in its dark palette #040706 #0b5d3d #0f8f7f #06261c at speed 0.4: full strength around the glass hero frame, in the AI patch and the closing frame, under a 65 % scrim behind the other sections.
6. Type: Clash Display 500 in sentence case for the hero and section titles (owner's pick over uppercase, 25 Oct: tracking 0, 0.98 leading, 84 px hero at 1440); only the GRIDZILLA wordmark is uppercase. General Sans 400/500/600 for everything else, tabular numbers. Both self-hosted, two faces preloaded.
7. Shape: frames 40 px, cards 24 px, every button a 980 px pill (primary white, secondary dark with the hairline). Depth from glass over the light, the inner hairline and a soft black drop, never from dividers.
8. Motion: the deck's slow light behind the page (lazy, idle-loaded, paused off screen and in a hidden tab), one entry moment on the hero (lines rise 24 px, 80 ms apart, frame settles from 0.98), reveals once per section, ticker once, hover lift on cards, press scale 0.98. Under reduced motion: no canvas (a static gradient in the same colours), nothing moves.
9. Rhythm: every section a different layout family (frame, editorial + chart, sticky split, bento, stat band, split tile, the AI light patch, grouped two columns, people row, closing frame); the closing frame mirrors the hero so the page is bookended.
10. Real imagery only: 2x webp screenshots of the real console (its dark theme) from a local platform (`npm run shots`), the real trailer, GitHub avatars, the real with/without chart as a live component.
