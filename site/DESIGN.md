# Gridzilla website: design note

1. Direction: one product page for grid operators, judges and partners, read as a Dribbble-grade product launch (the owner's Vexo shot), not an "energy website". Light, white and black, one orange. Dials: variance 8, motion 6, density 3.
2. Borrowed from Vexo (Muhammad Shofiuddoula): the huge 40 px frame with a cool grey gradient inside, giant uppercase type, a dark portrait card at the centre of the frame (ours is the console in a thin dark bezel rising from the frame's bottom edge), the black pill with a round arrow badge.
3. Borrowed from 21st.dev: the inverted-curve notch of "Adaptive Notch Navigation Bar" (@arunachalam) for the nav tab cut into the hero frame; the spring count of "Number Ticker" (@dillionverma) for the proof numbers, changed to render the final value first so it is never empty; the sticky split of "Sticky Content Wrapper" (@hyperiux) for How it works, rebuilt with IntersectionObserver instead of GSAP.
4. Borrowed from Dribbble search "modern website": the small rounded video card at the hero's bottom right (Piqo Studio, Shofiuddoula's other shots) and the device rising out of a frame ("New era of finance", Phenomenon Studio).
5. Palette (locked): page #ffffff, tint #f5f5f7, ink #1d1d1f, secondary #6e6e73, tertiary #86868b (large text and lines only), fill #e8e8ed, press #d2d2d7, hero frame #e9eef3 to #f6f8fa. Accent #ff9500 only on the live dot, the eaten peak, link hover underlines and one proof number. Chart colours live only inside charts.
6. Type: Clash Display 600 uppercase for the hero and section titles (tight, -0.02em, 0.92 leading), General Sans 400/500/600 for everything else, tabular numbers. Both self-hosted, two faces preloaded.
7. Shape: frames 40 px, cards 24 px, every button a 980 px pill. Depth from cool-tinted shadows (rgb 29 39 52), never from borders; the white pill gets one inset hairline.
8. Motion: one entry moment on the hero (lines rise 24 px, 80 ms apart, frame settles from 0.98), reveals once per section, ticker once, hover lift on cards, press scale 0.98. No loops except the live dot; all of it collapses under reduced motion.
9. Rhythm: every section a different layout family (frame, editorial + chart, sticky split, bento, stat band, split tile, the one dark panel, grouped two columns, people row, closing frame); the closing frame mirrors the hero so the page is bookended.
10. Real imagery only: 2x webp screenshots of the real console from a local platform (`npm run shots`), the real trailer, GitHub avatars, the real with/without chart as a live component.
