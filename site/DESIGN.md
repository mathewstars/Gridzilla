# Gridzilla website: design note

1. Direction: one product page for grid operators, judges and partners, read as a Dribbble-grade product launch (the owner's Vexo shot), not an "energy website". Black and green like the team's deck (owner, 25 Oct: "ours is black and green and super premium"), one theme, locked. Dials: variance 8, motion 6, density 3.
2. Borrowed from Vexo (Muhammad Shofiuddoula): giant display type (we tried Vexo's uppercase; the owner picked sentence case), a dark device at the centre (ours is the console in a thin dark bezel rising from the hero's bottom edge, at most 1180 px), the pill with a round arrow badge. Vexo's rounded hero frame was dropped in round 2 (owner: "like a bubble"): the hero is full bleed like the deck's title slide, the nav a plain bar that turns into a thin glass strip on scroll.
3. Borrowed from 21st.dev: the spring count of "Number Ticker" (@dillionverma) for the proof numbers, changed to render the final value first so it is never empty; the sticky split of "Sticky Content Wrapper" (@hyperiux) for How it works, rebuilt with IntersectionObserver instead of GSAP.
4. Borrowed from Dribbble search "modern website": the small rounded video card beside the device (Piqo Studio, Shofiuddoula's other shots; ours sits 24 px right of the bezel, never over it, and moves under the pills below 1280 px) and the device rising out of the hero ("New era of finance", Phenomenon Studio).
5. Palette (locked, the deck's dark theme from dashboard/src/pitch/pitch.css): ground #0b0b0f, tile #1d1d1f at 92 % with an inner #2a2a2e hairline, quiet tile #141418, ink #f5f5f7, secondary #a1a1a6, tertiary #8e8e93, fill #2a2a2e, press #3a3a3e. Accent green #34c759 only on the live dot, link hover underlines, icons in the AI patch and the one proof number. Chart colours live only inside charts. Behind everything the deck's Mesh Gradient (21st.dev @paper-design, copied unchanged) in its dark palette #040706 #0b5d3d #0f8f7f #06261c at speed 0.4: full strength in the hero, nearly full in the AI patch and the closing call, under a 65 % scrim behind the other sections.
6. Type: Clash Display 500 in sentence case for the hero and section titles (owner's pick over uppercase, 25 Oct: tracking 0, 0.98 leading, 84 px hero at 1440); only the GRIDZILLA wordmark is uppercase. General Sans 400/500/600 for everything else, tabular numbers. Both self-hosted, two faces preloaded.
7. Shape and layout: one content width, 1240 px centred, every section title on its left edge; sections 120 px top and bottom from 1024 px, 72 on the phone, nothing stacked on that; tiles 24 px corners with 32 px inside (24 on the phone); rounded corners only on the console bezel, the trailer card and tiles; every button a 980 px pill (primary white, secondary dark with the hairline), 44 px tap targets on the phone. Depth from glass over the light, the inner hairline and a soft black drop, never from dividers.
8. Motion: the deck's slow light behind the page (lazy, idle-loaded, paused off screen and in a hidden tab), one entry moment on the hero (lines rise 24 px, 80 ms apart, the console rises last), reveals once per section, ticker once, hover lift on cards, press scale 0.98. Under 768 px and under reduced motion: no canvas (a static gradient in the same colours), nothing moves.
9. Rhythm: every section a different layout family (full-bleed hero, editorial + chart, sticky split, signal explorer, stat band, split tile, the AI light patch, a 3 + 2 grid, people row, closing call); the full-bleed closing call mirrors the hero so the page is bookended.
10. Real imagery only: 2x webp screenshots of the real console (its dark theme) from a local platform (`npm run shots`), the real trailer, GitHub avatars, the real with/without chart as a live component.
